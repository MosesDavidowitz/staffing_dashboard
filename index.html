Address lookup for testing

The reports are read in your browser. To show map pins, this demo sends street addresses near the selected patient (street, city, state, ZIP only) to the public US Census Bureau geocoder when you search, and to OpenStreetMap Nominatim only if the Census cannot find an address. Names, IDs, phones and schedules are not included in those lookup requests. Map tiles come from OpenStreetMap and Esri. Do not use real patient or caregiver addresses without agency approval and an appropriate privacy arrangement.
