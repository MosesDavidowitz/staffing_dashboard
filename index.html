Address lookup for testing

The reports are read in your browser. To show map pins, this demo sends selected patient and caregiver street addresses to the public OpenStreetMap Nominatim address service when you search. Names, IDs and schedules are not included in those lookup requests. Map tiles are also requested from OpenStreetMap. Do not use real patient or caregiver addresses without agency approval and an appropriate privacy arrangement.
