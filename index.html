Upload HHAXchange data
Use three CSV exports. Tap ⓘ for report instructions and required columns. Report menu names vary by HHAXchange account and permissions.
Patient roster / patient demographic export

In HHAXchange, look under Reports for a patient list, census, or demographics export. The exact menu path and report name have not been verified for your account.

Filters: Active patients; include all service locations and ZIP codes you intend to staff. Export CSV.

Required CSV headers: id,name,address,lat,lng. Optional: zip. Each patient needs map coordinates. HHAXchange may not provide them.

Caregiver roster / caregiver demographics export

In HHAXchange, look under Reports for caregiver list or demographics. Exact menu path is account-dependent and not verified.

Filters: Active caregivers, across all relevant branches or offices. Include home address. Export CSV.

Required CSV headers: id,name,address,lat,lng. Coordinates may need to be added separately.

Scheduled visits / visit calendar export

In HHAXchange, look under Reports for a scheduled visits or visit detail export. Exact report name and menu path have not been verified.

Filters: Include all assigned visits for the staffing period, ideally the next 30 days. Include scheduled, assigned visits and relevant branches; don't limit to completed visits. Export CSV.

Required CSV headers: caregiver_id,patient_id,date,start,end. Date: YYYY-MM-DD; times: HH:MM (24-hour). IDs must match the other files.

Important: Raw HHAXchange exports may need column renaming and geocoding before this version can load them. Do not upload real patient information to an unapproved public site.
